About this site

This website is a companion to the PhD dissertation Designing for Participatory Music-Making by Lucia Montesinos, submitted at IT University of Copenhagen in 2026.

Throughout the thesis, certain passages are accompanied by audiovisual material that complement or extend the written argument. This site collects all of that material in one place. You will find videos, audio recordings, and interactive 3D models. The 3D models are best viewed on a computer, and they are meant to illustrate some of the main interaction premisses of the artifacts, so they don't fully represent the artefacts.

How to navigate

The best way to use this website is by reading along the text and navigating through the figures when indicated by the \faGlobe, which will link to the corresponding figure. You can also navigate through the figures using the arrows, or the numbers. GO TO LIST OF FIGURES.
