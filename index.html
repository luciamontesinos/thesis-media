About this site

This website is a companion to the PhD dissertation by Lucia Montesinos.

Throughout the thesis, certain passages are accompanied by audiovisual material — recordings, images, and video excerpts — that complement or extend the written argument. This site collects all of that material in one place.

How to navigate

Each figure in the thesis carries a QR code or short URL. Scanning or following it opens that specific item directly. Once on the site, you can move through the full collection using the Prev and Next buttons, or jump to any item from the list of figures.
